Overview

What we do

Packing graded pebbles in the annulus around the casing does two quiet but important jobs: it filters fine sand and silt before they reach the pump, and it stabilises the borewell wall.

Done with the right grade and quantity, gravel packing extends casing life, protects the pump, and keeps the water coming clean.

What's included

Every gravel & pebble packing job covers

  • Graded, washed pebble media
  • Correct annulus packing depth
  • Silt and fine-sand filtration
  • Casing stabilisation
  • Home, commercial and industrial bores
  • Supplied and placed by our crew

When it's the right call

Best suited for

  • New borewells in sandy strata
  • Bores drawing silty or sandy water
  • Protecting a new submersible pump
  • Deep high-yield borewells
  • Any bore prone to sand ingress

Questions

Frequently asked

What do pebbles do in a borewell?

Graded pebbles packed around the casing act as a filter — they hold back sand and silt while letting water through — and they support the borewell wall.

Will gravel packing stop sandy water?

In sand-prone strata it makes a real difference, filtering fines before they reach your pump. We match the pebble grade to your soil for the best result.
