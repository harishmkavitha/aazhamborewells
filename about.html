Our story

Named for depth, built on trust

Aazham Borewells was founded in 1979 by a family that believed borewell work should be done honestly: survey before you drill, tell the customer the truth about their ground, and put the estimate in writing.

Over 45 years we've drilled through every kind of strata Chennai can throw at a bit — soft coastal sand, stubborn granite, mixed rock — and added the machines and methods to match: lorry rigs, DTH hammers, compressors and compact Galaxy units for tight city plots.

Today we handle the whole life of a borewell, from the first survey to cleaning one that's decades old. Every job is done by our own people, because that's the only way to stand behind it.
