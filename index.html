Who we are

Two generations of getting Chennai its water

Aazham Borewells began in 1979 with a single rig and a simple promise: survey honestly, drill properly, and stand behind the result. Three decades on, that hasn't changed — we've just gone deeper, added cleaning and pump work, and kept every job in-house.

The word aazham means depth in Tamil. It's what we're named for and what we're good at: reading the ground, choosing the right method, and going down until the water's there.

How we work

Survey first. Quote in writing. Then drill.

  1. Survey the ground

    A point survey fixes the best spot and a realistic depth before any machine moves.

  2. Quote in writing

    Diameter, casing, per-foot rate and terms — agreed on paper, no surprises later.

  3. Drill with the right method

    Rig, DTH, compressor or Galaxy, matched to your soil and access.

  4. Test & hand over clean

    We flush, check the yield, clear the site and leave you a depth record.
