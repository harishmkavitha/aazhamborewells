DTH Method Drilling in Chennai

Down-the-Hole (DTH) hammer drilling for fast, straight borewells through Chennai's hard granite formations.

Overview

What we do

DTH drilling drives a pneumatic hammer at the base of the drill string, breaking rock directly at the face. It's the most effective method through the hard granite and gneiss common under much of Chennai.

Because the hammer sits down the hole, bores stay straight and true even at depth — which matters for pump installation and long-term casing life.

What's included

Every dth method drilling job covers

  • Efficient through hard rock
  • Straight, plumb bores at depth
  • Higher penetration rates
  • Lorry-mounted DTH available
  • Clean cuttings removal by air
  • Suited to deep, high-yield borewells

How the work runs

A clear process, start to finish

  1. Assess the formation

    We confirm rock type and depth so the hammer and bit are matched to the ground.

  2. Position the rig

    The DTH rig is set and centred over the surveyed point.

  3. Hammer to depth

    Compressed air drives the hammer; cuttings are lifted clear as the bore advances.

  4. Case & develop

    Casing is set through the top soil and the bore is flushed and yield-checked.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is DTH suitable for rocky Chennai soil?

Yes — DTH is the standard method for the hard rock found across much of Chennai and generally the fastest way through granite.

Does DTH keep the borehole straight?

Because the hammer works at the bottom of the hole, DTH bores stay notably straighter than some rotary methods, which helps pump alignment later.
